HELEN FALLS IN

West Byfleet → Godalming, along the Wey
Set in the Surrey countryside, UK
🚲 🌾 🦆 🍺

Helen is cycling the towpath to Godalming, calling at the pubs on the way. The whole ride takes about ten minutes — if she stays out of the water.

Falling in is allowed: climb out, carry on. Reach a pub and you can start from it next time — what Helen drinks there is up to you.

This game is a small meditation. (For you. Perhaps less so for Helen!)
