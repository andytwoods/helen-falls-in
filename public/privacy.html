HELEN FALLS IN — PRIVACY POLICY

Helen Falls In is a free game by Andy Woods, available on the web and as an Android app.

Data collection

The game collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the game is sent anywhere.

Local storage

Your progress (unlocked pubs and personal bests) is stored only on your own device, in local storage. It never leaves your device, and uninstalling the app (or clearing your browser's site data) deletes it.

Network access

The Android app is fully self-contained and works offline. The web version downloads the game from this site and nothing more.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, children included.
