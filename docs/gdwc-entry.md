## SHORT DESCRIPTION (final)

Steer Helen's wobbly bike along a Surrey canal towpath — tap left or right to
lean — and try to reach the next pub without ending up in the water. Free, runs in a browser,
takes about ten minutes.

## LONG DESCRIPTION (final)

Helen Falls In is a two-thumb cycling game set on the Wey Navigation, which
runs from West Byfleet to Godalming. I live nearby. The pubs are real: The
Anchor at Pyrford Lock, The New Inn at Send, The Parrot at Shalford. They're
also checkpoints — reach one and you can start there next time. You can buy
Helen a drink, which makes her faster and much worse at cycling. Lemonade
sobers her up.

There's only one control: tap left or right to lean, while the bike wobbles
and drifts towards the canal. Falling in isn't game over — Helen climbs out
and drips for a bit. I balanced the handling with a headless test rig that
plays 200 seeded runs after every change. Mash the screen and she's usually
underwater within four seconds; careful, late corrections get her through the
whole ten-minute ride.

Everything on screen is made in code — the pixel art, the birdsong, the
hecklers — so there are no asset files in the repo. I used an AI assistant
heavily to build it. The game's concept, design and setting are my own.
